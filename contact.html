Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Send a request using the form on this page or call us at (519) 326-3331. We'll confirm your time as soon as possible.

What should I expect on my first visit?

Please arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in. We'll chat about your goals and any concerns before your treatment begins.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes! Gift cards are available for any amount or a specific treatment. Call us or stop by the spa to purchase one.

What is the Halo salt cave?

It's a relaxing wellness experience: fine, dry salt particles are dispersed into our Salt Room during a 30-minute session while you sit back and breathe.

Are you open late?

Yes — we're open until 8:00pm on Thursdays, making it easy to fit in a treatment after work.
