Welcome to Lavender

A calm escape, right here in Leamington

As the only full-service spa in Leamington, we give you the outlet you need to find serenity in your busy life.

Our team is dedicated to delivering exceptional experiences through advanced treatments and personalized care. Whether you're stopping in for a quick polish, a restorative massage or a full afternoon of pampering, you'll be welcomed like a friend.

  • Unique wellness experiences — Halo salt cave & European-style head spa
